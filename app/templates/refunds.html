Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Wave Cinema (test build)

Test mode

Right now the site is in test mode: no real money is taken, so there's nothing to refund. Any checkout flow you see is a rehearsal.

When payments go live

  • Credits have no cash value and are non-transferable. Unused credits aren't refundable once spent on a generation.
  • Failed generations: if our cloud lane charges you and produces nothing usable, we'll re-credit the generation. That's only fair.
  • Change of mind: credit packs are non-refundable once purchased, except where consumer law says otherwise.
  • Your own account (DIY lane): costs nothing here, so nothing to refund here. Any spend on your own video-gen account is between you and them.

How to ask

When live billing starts, refund requests go through the same channel you got your invite from. Include your project ID and what happened.
