Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Wave Cinema (test build)

What we collect

  • Your uploads: songs, clips and the videos we assemble. That's the whole point of the app.
  • Project data: your film names, scene plans, and which takes you crowned.
  • Payment info: handled by Stripe, never stored on our servers. In test mode, none of it's real anyway.
  • Basic technical bits: the boring stuff servers log (IP, browser type) to keep things running.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data. Ever.
  • We don't train AI models on your songs.
  • We don't share your uploads with anyone except the generation providers you choose (your lane, your call).

Cookies

We use the bare minimum to keep you logged into your session. No ad trackers, no creepy pixels.

Your rights

Want your stuff deleted? Ask and it's gone: projects, uploads, everything. This is a test build, so data may also be wiped during rebuilds without warning.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reach out through the same channel you got your invite link from.
