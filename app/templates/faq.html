FAQ

Everything people ask before their first film.

What is Wave Cinema?

An AI film studio for your music. Drop in a song, direct the vision, generate the clips scene by scene, crown the winners, and ship a finished music video. No camera crew, no budget meetings.

How does it work?

Four steps: Drop the Track (upload your song) → Direct the Film (we slice it into scenes and plan every shot) → Pick Your Shots (generate or upload clips, crown the winners) → Ship It (we stitch it all together under your song).

Do I need my own video-generation account?

No, but it's the free route. DIY Clips means you generate on your own account and upload the clips back. Costs nothing. Wave Cinema Cloud means we generate for you using your credits (coming soon).

What does it cost?

Planning, directing and assembly are free. DIY Clips is free (you spend on your own account, if anything). Cloud generations use credits when that lane opens. The site is currently in test mode, so no real money moves.

What audio can I upload?

MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG or FLAC, up to 250 MB. If your track is in another format, convert it first. Any free converter will do.

Who owns the finished video?

You do. It's your song and your direction. Just make sure you actually own the rights to the song (the checkbox at upload isn't decoration).

What if a clip looks wrong?

That's what Pick Your Shots is for. AI misfires sometimes: warped hands, drifting faces, mystery text. Don't crown it. Reroll the scene or generate a fresh take.

Is my music safe?

Your uploads are only used to make your film. We don't sell data, we don't train models on your songs. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does it say "test mode"?

Because friends and early testers are kicking the tyres before we open the doors properly. Payments are simulated, and the odd bug is part of the deal. Tell us when you find one.
